Follow the money through InsureMO

BCP (Billing, Collection and Payment) is the finance module of InsureMO. Every policy, endorsement, claim, reinsurance and commission transaction that has an amount ends up here as a receivable (AR), a payable (AP) or a fee. BCP collects the money, pays the money, settles the balances and feeds the General Ledger.

Built from the InsureMO BCP module guide. Content marked Domain context is general insurance practice added to help you learn. It is not taken from the source guide, so confirm it with your project before you rely on it.

The three money movements in BCP
Money in
Bill (AR)→Collect→Suspense→Offset→Settled
Money out
Bill (AP)→Approve→Confirm payment→Settled
Internal
AR + AP→Net off / tolerance / write-off→Settled

Every step creates fee records. Fees drive the GL posting. Every step except Cancel can be reversed.

Level 1

Foundations

What BCP does, the words it uses, and where it sits in the InsureMO landscape.

What BCP does

1. Billing

Receives financial data from PA, Claims, Reinsurance, Sales Channel and Printing through the billing API (syncDataToBCP). Each call creates a bill with one or more AR or AP records and a fee breakdown.

2. Collection and payment

Receives money (collection) and pays money (refund / payment). Settles AR and AP through offset. Supports reversal of every step except cancel.

3. Query and GL feed

Lets users search every financial record in any status and export to Excel. Produces fee records that become GL vouchers for the accounting system.

Why it matters: in most implementations BCP is a middle system. It records each money movement as a fee entry and passes it to a third-party accounting system for reconciliation and reporting. Errors in BCP become errors in the books.

Two ways to collect money

One step

Collect and offset together

Premium Collection and Single Collection. The cashier picks the AR, records the receipt, and the system settles the AR at once.

Typical for customer billing and personal lines.

Two steps

Collect to suspense, apply later

Suspense Collection, then Application. Money is parked in a suspense account and later used to settle one or many ARs.

Typical for agent billing, coinsurers and commercial lines. Gives flexibility when one remittance covers many policies.

Level 2

Money lifecycles and status models

Every scenario in BCP is a variation of three patterns. Learn the patterns and the status changes, and the scenarios become easy.

The three patterns

Money in

Collect → Suspense → Offset

  1. Source module bills BCP. AR is created as Outstanding.
  2. Money is received. A receipt is created and held in suspense.
  3. The receipt is offset against the AR. AR balance becomes 0 and status Settled.

Reverse in the opposite order: reverse the offset first, then reverse the collection.

Money out

Approve → Confirm

  1. Source module bills BCP. AP is created as Outstanding.
  2. Finance approves. A payment record is created as Approved.
  3. Payment is confirmed after the bank transfer. Payment becomes Paid and AP is settled.

Reverse: one step, Reverse Refund. AP returns to Outstanding.

Internal

Net, tolerate or write off

  1. ARAP Offset nets AR and AP of the same party. No money moves.
  2. Tolerance Offset clears small differences.
  3. Write-off closes bad debt.

Reverse: Reverse Application & Offset.

Why payments need approval and collections do not: paying money out is the higher-risk direction. Finance staff decide which APs can be paid and which are in dispute before money leaves the company. The guide calls refund "a very sensitive process".

Partial and over payment

Over-collection

Paid more than the AR

  • AR is fully settled.
  • The excess stays in the suspense account.
  • The excess can be applied to another AR of the same payer, refunded with Receipt Refund, or kept for later.
Under-collection

Paid less than the AR

  • AR status does not change. It stays Outstanding.
  • Only the balance goes down by the amount offset.
  • More partial payments can follow. When the total offset equals the AR amount, the AR becomes Settled.

Installments, multiple payers and multiple payees

The billing call decides how many AR or AP records are created. Every process then runs per record.

CombinationInstallmentPayer / payeeAR / AP created
①❶SingleSingleOne AR or AP
②❶InstallmentSingleOne AR per period
①❷SingleMultipleAR split by payer / AP split by payee
②❷InstallmentMultiplePeriods × payers (or payees)

Exceptions: Reinsurance has no installments (TotalPeriods is always 1). Claim recovery generally has no installments and no multiple payers. Screens still show one row per transaction and display "Current Period" and "Total Periods".

Level 6 · Advanced

Fees, GL and offset logic

ARAP drives collection and payment. Fees drive accounting. Every time an AR or AP amount changes, BCP writes fee records that the GL turns into journal entries.

ARAP and fee: two different records

ARAP

The amount actually owed to or by an external party. Drives collection and payment. ArapCate = 1 is AR, ArapCate = 2 is AP.

Fee

A detailed record of each money movement, used for GL posting. Every billing call creates fees, but not every fee has an ARAP. Example: a claim reserve creates a fee only (200201), with no collection or payment.

Transaction TypeWhat produced the fee: NEWBIZ, ENDORSEMENT, COLLECTION, COLLECTION_CONFIRM, OFFSET, PAYMENT, PAYMENT_CONFIRMATION, SALESCHANNEL, COMMISSION.

Partial payment: how fees are offset

When an installment is paid in part, BCP must decide which fees inside the AR (premium, tax, each coverage) the payment settles. Two methods exist. Priority offset uses the FeeTypePriority data table and settles fees in priority order. Proportional offset splits the payment by each fee's share. Priority wins. If only some fee types have a priority, those are settled first and the rest of the payment is split proportionally (mixed mode). The installment (ARAP) offset fee always equals the amount paid.

Configuration, APIs and extension

What you can change with configuration, what the platform API already does, and what each implementation must build in the iComposer layer.

Platform API or iComposer?

The BCP core API already handles the common, multi-tenant flows: ARAP, collection, payment, offset, fee, status changes and reversals. Client needs beyond that differ by market and are built in an iComposer layer, on the client's tech stack or on the InsureMO app framework.

UploadBulk collection upload through ExceliComposer
Fit-gap tip: tolerance thresholds and payment authority limits are not listed as configuration. Plan them as build items unless the platform team confirms otherwise.

What you can configure

Data model

All objects and fields are in the Data Dictionary (Module = BCP, Group = BCP_Core, Context = Common), covering BizTransaction, ARAP, Commission, Invoice, Prepaid Order, Collection and Offset. Tenants can add fields, for example ARAPSequence or AccountHolderIdNo on BcpArap. Fields added to the DD and passed in the API appear in the query detail view without code changes. Changes must be deployed. Export and import work only between environments on the same platform version.

Code and data tables

Drive drop-down lists and validate API payloads. Some tables are fixed by the platform: ask the platform team before changing BcpArapType, BcpOffsetType or BcpCollectionStatus.

Numbering

Receipt, payment, offset and statement numbers come from numbering templates and can be changed.

Search index

Index Management controls which fields are searchable, exact-match only, or displayed. ARAP index: schema bcp_arap, read alias bcp_arap_r_alias, write alias bcp_arap_w_alias5, rebuild batch arapIndexRebuildBatch. Number and date fields must have the correct type, or range search and sorting will fail.

Messages

Validation messages are configured per language.

BCPConfig parameters

arapFieldAllowUpdate: which ARAP fields users can edit in ARAP Management.
bcp.export.gl.columns: columns and order of the GL export, for example GLVoucherNo,PolicyNo,GLDate,Amount,GLStatus,Branch.

Tabs for ARAP, Collection and Payment. Filters: ARAP Type, Product, Branch (with subordinates), Entry Date (default: the full current year). 32 extra filter fields can be added.

Custom report: Group Type (text fields) with Average and Sum (number fields).
